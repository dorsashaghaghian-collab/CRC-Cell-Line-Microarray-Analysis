# GEO Dataset Screening Summary

This table summarizes GEO studies examined during the dataset-screening stage of the colorectal cancer transcriptomic project.

The purpose of screening was to identify datasets containing experimentally relevant colorectal cancer models and to distinguish suitable cell-line studies from datasets with incompatible biological systems or experimental designs.

> **Important:** The studies listed below represent datasets examined during the screening process. Inclusion in this screening table does not imply that every study was used in downstream analysis.

## Screening Criteria

Each candidate study was evaluated using several criteria:

- colorectal cancer relevance
- organism
- biological model
- cell-line identity
- sample type
- experimental condition
- sequencing or transcriptomic platform
- sample availability
- metadata quality
- experimental group structure
- compatibility with the research question

---

## Cell-Line Eligibility

A dataset was considered potentially relevant to the cell-line component when the experimental material consisted of colorectal cancer cell models and the dataset contained sufficient sample-level information for interpretation.

Datasets representing fundamentally different biological systems were not treated as interchangeable with cell-line experiments.

---

## Data Availability

The availability of expression measurements and corresponding sample metadata was assessed before downstream analysis.

Expression data were considered usable only when the relationship between samples, experimental groups, and expression measurements could be established with sufficient confidence.

---

## Interpretation of the Screening Process

Dataset screening was an important analytical step rather than a simple keyword-based search.

The screening process reduced the risk of:

- mixing primary tissue and cell-line data
- comparing incompatible experimental conditions
- incorrectly assigning biological groups
- interpreting unrelated experimental datasets as equivalent
- introducing technical or biological heterogeneity without justification

---

## Reproducibility

All datasets are identified using their original GEO accession numbers.

The accession numbers provide a direct link to the original public studies and allow the dataset-selection process to be independently reviewed.
